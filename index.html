Chaos Island

A tiny floating island. You control the weather. You bring the chaos.

  • Drag to look around the island
  • Scroll to zoom in and out
  • Click to throw stuff, call lightning or blow a gust. Pick a tool at the bottom
  • Pull the time lever or press R to undo the chaos
  • Use the weather machine for time, rain, snow and wind
  • Secret: try snow in the middle of the night
